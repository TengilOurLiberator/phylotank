# src/ui design notes (phase 1)

What exists, where it lives, and the decisions taken overnight without William. Design reference: [07-ux](../sections/07-ux.md); contract: CONTRACT 13, 14.2, 15.

## Decisions

1. **Plain DOM, no framework.** A 40-line `h()` helper, an `Emitter`, and Canvas 2D for every chart. The whole app is 75 KB gzipped (spike ui-1). Preact, signals and uPlot (07 §0.3) were not needed; nothing was added to `package.json`.
2. **The god window runs on a mock by default.** `god.html` with no `?host=` and not served by the host uses `MockSimLink` in the page. `?sim=mock` gives the screensaver and TV the mock too (so captions can be seen); `?sim=worker` forces the dream-tank worker. Wallpaper, screensaver and TV keep the worker by default. The mock labels itself in the top bar ("mock sim") and in Settings.
3. **Captions.** Only events the director would show are captioned (hunt start, speciation, extinction, giant, god, release, school start, mass death, frenzy, outbreak start, bloom front, migration, new role), scored with `BASE_INTEREST`. Each caption is a whole template with the species name as a slot rendered in italics. A speed change produces one "Time is running Nx faster" caption (Q2: never fast-forward silently). The wallpaper never creates a caption element, so its DOM has no text (checked by `tests/ui-screens.e2e.cjs`).
4. **Speed and the overnight lease.** Buttons for pause, 0.25x, 1x, 4x, 16x and max (DESIGN R29). Anything but 1x is renewed every 10 s; the mock lapses it 30 s after the last renewal. "Run at max until…" sends `{speed:'max', until:{wallIso}, unattended:true}`, which the mock honours until the time passes even with the window gone; the top bar shows "Max until 07:00 (unattended)" with a Stop button while it runs, and a banner explains the lease for ordinary fast-forward.
5. **Powers.** The palette shows the core 12 (CONTRACT 13). Major (by core `isMajorPower`) needs a 1.2 s hold, with "Confirm without holding" as the accessible alternative and Enter-hold on the keyboard. The host takes the `beforePower` snapshot and returns `snapshotId`; if a result has none, the client takes one itself (still inside the 3 s lead). The countdown (3 s major, 1 s minor) pauses while the world is paused. A stale-epoch rejection says "World was rewound; power not cast." Species-targeted powers use a checkbox list; circle and point powers target by clicking the tank, with a live "In range" count from the dust frame.
6. **Inspector.** Picking is on the CPU from the last DUST_FRAME, with BODY_ENTER giving slot to entity (the mock streams every creature as a dust mote with the same slot). Brain view: inputs left, outputs right, hidden in the middle, dashed negative edges, thickness |w|, brightness from INSPECT; clicking a node starts a 20 s trace.
7. **Designer.** One genome in bytes; every edit is decode, change, canonical encode, so the two editors cannot drift. Sliders are generated from `TRAITS` and `BRAIN_TRAITS` by their schema group (hidden traits go to Advanced, so nothing heritable is hidden); brain groups carry "starting instinct, may evolve". The part editor edits rules: click a part to select the rule that grew it (all copies highlight), add parts from the palette to the selected rule, edit module fields and link fields, break out one copy, duplicate, delete. The growth strip scrubs maturity 0 to 1 and plays over 6 s. Diet has a barycentric triangle picker over the enzyme simplex, next to its two sliders.
8. **Bodies before `develop` exists.** `growBody` calls `genome.develop` and falls back to a local sketch of the body graph, labelled "sketch preview" on the canvas. The sketch is view-only.
9. **Test tank is a toy.** There is no sim test tank yet, so the panel animates the grown body and reports speeds from the budget model, labelled "toy model until the sim test tank lands". Honest numbers (07 §0.1) means it must be replaced, not tuned.
10. **Energy budget.** CONTRACT 9 shapes and coefficients (Kleiber 0.75 with Arrhenius, repair `C_REP`, brain `e_n`, `e_s`, Holling II with `J_max = 8B`, enzyme simplex, clutch overhead). Always labelled an estimate. Trade-off chips: central differences at ±5% of range over 13 lever loci, computed 250 ms after edits pause; the three largest show as "+10%: net −16%" and jump to their slider. Validation never disables Release; with an inviable issue the button reads "Release anyway".
11. **Templates.** Meadowlamp, Hushmaw, Sievebell, Tetherling, Ashpicker, Deepcrown and Motefly (Q9), plus Blank. Motefly is a 1.2 mm photosynthetic cilia mote (*Motilux errans*, original). `genome.template(id)` wins when it is implemented; these local ones are placeholders for the genome engineer's tuned versions.
12. **Release as leviathan.** A confirmed major action: hold or confirm in a dialog that explains the endowment; the UI sets `GENOME_FLAG_LEVIATHAN` in the genome and sends `asLeviathan: true`; the host snapshots first.
13. **Library.** Templates are read-only entries; designs, captures and imports live in this viewer's `localStorage` as `PTK1.` strings (the host library folder replaces it later). De-duplication by xxh64 content id; fingerprints are the SHA-256 prefix (xxh64 fallback where SubtleCrypto is missing). Imports from paste, drop or file go through size caps, CRC, strict CBOR, genome decode and `sanitiseText`, and always open in the designer first.
14. **QR codes** are encoded locally (byte mode, ECC L, versions 1 to 40) and shown only when the string is under 2,300 characters.
15. **Host first for the wallpaper.** `wallpaper.html` (the Lively page) uses `HostFirstLink`: the host socket first, the dream-tank worker after 2 s of silence, probes with 0.5, 1, 2, 4, then 10 s backoff, back to the host when it answers, back to the worker if the host goes silent for 5 s. `index.html` keeps the plain worker (the browser demo, and what the smoke test checks). Every view sends VIEWPORT when its camera moves (at most 10 Hz) and ACK at 2 Hz.
16. **Keyboard.** 07 §6.2's map (Space, 1 to 5, arrows, + and -, F, I, G, Del, M, P, D, L, S, T/B, Esc, Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Shift+Z, Ctrl+S, Ctrl+B, Ctrl+,) plus C (inspect the creature nearest the view centre) and N / Shift+N (jump to the next or previous located event). Powers that target a place have a "Target the view centre" button, so the whole cast works without a pointer.
17. **Language** follows `navigator.language`, `?lang=` overrides (`?lang=xx` is the pseudo-locale: accented, 35% longer, used to find clipping), and Settings or the top-bar menu switches without a reload. The Swedish strings are a first pass for William to review.

## Second pass (mod/ui2)

New files: `src/ui/labels.ts` (slider labels), `src/ui/i18n2.ts` (second-pass messages, merged into `i18n.ts`), `src/ui/naming.ts` (rename rules and the NameBook), `src/ui/onboarding.ts`, `src/ui/gamepad.ts`, `src/ui/tv.ts`, `src/ui/god/knobs.ts`, `src/ui/god/foodweb.ts`; `drawDrift` in `charts.ts`.

18. **Slider labels are a table, not the schema.** `labels.ts` maps every TRAITS, BRAIN_TRAITS, group and part-field name to English and Swedish (DRIVE_* and RULEn_* are composed). An unlabelled future schema name falls back to a tidied identifier, so a new trait still gets a slider; `missingLabels()` lists gaps and a test keeps it empty. The inspector's gene table uses the same labels. One English label was shortened to keep the 150 px column ("Reserve before breeding").
19. **William's renames win.** A rename is validated (`checkRename`: case is fixed for the user, length and letters are refused, a duplicate only warns), written to a per-world NameBook (this viewer's localStorage mirror) and sent as `setting` `species.<id>.name` until the contract has `rename` (contract request 8). The NameBook is applied over every species list the client receives (STATS, CENSUS, WELCOME) and over captions in the screensaver and TV, so the user name shows at once and survives a host that refuses the key or later sends another generated name. Daughters inherit a renamed ancestor's genus while their generated genus matches it. Whole-genus rename is a checkbox. Rename is reachable from the inspector, the stats species table (the phylogeny's table view) and the map's species list; a library rename (design metadata) is not done.
20. **Knobs.** 22 `KnobSpec`s from 07 §3.9 (the speciation threshold is left to its owner), presets Gentle, Harsh, Fast evolution, Custom, per-knob reset and "Reset all to preset". A knob change is sent on slider release; each row shows sending, applied, or "this host does not have this knob yet" (`unknown-setting` or `not-implemented`) instead of pretending. New-world knobs (size, seed) are held until "Start a new world with these" (`world.new`). The mock applies plankton growth, metabolic cost and the mutation multiplier to its toy ecology and logs each change as a timeline event.
21. **Food web.** The host sends flows (contract request 11); the UI builds nodes (species plus plankton, plants, detritus), solves `(I - W^T) TL = 1` by Gaussian elimination, lays x out with a 1-D force layout seeded by the previous frame (y is fixed by TL), and draws log-flow edge widths, dotted parasitism, dashed scavenging and arrowheads at the eater. Labels move below a node when they would overlap. The table view lists levels and flows.
22. **Gene drift heat map.** The client keeps each `STATS.genes` histogram as a time series per trait and species (720 samples, reset on a new epoch); the Genes tab toggles "Now" (histograms) and "Drift over time" (time on x, value on y, column-normalised brightness, axis labels in trait units and sim days). The mock random-walks its species' genes and makes Deepcrown's adult size creep up, as in 07 §3.6's example.
23. **Onboarding.** The 07 §5.2 machine is a pure `step()` with tests; the god window shows a non-blocking welcome card 1.5 s after the tank first has 50 creatures (07 §5.1 says about 10 s; a sooner card felt right with a tank this fast), then coach marks for inspect (C on the keyboard), Algae bloom (Powers opens with it picked) and the designer (Meadowlamp loaded), then the views card. Skip, Just watch, 20 s idle, and Start empty (confirmed; `world.new` with `empty`) all work by keyboard. Flags are in localStorage (`pt.onboarding`); "Show the welcome and tour again" is in Settings and a "?" in the top bar. The tour is off under `navigator.webdriver` unless `?onboarding=on`, so automated tests are not intercepted. `index.html` (the plain worker demo) has no onboarding yet: the god window is the public front door in browser-only mode.
24. **TV overlay.** `tv.ts`: Hidden, Shown(focus), Hidden after 8 s or on Back. The card controls only this TV's camera and sound: Director, Follow (tracks the creature nearest the frame centre by stable dust slot), Next event, Zoom in and out, Captions on or off, Sound 0 to 10, Back. Remote: arrows move focus (left and right walk every control, up and down change row), Enter is OK, Back is any of Escape, Backspace, GoBack, BrowserBack. Gamepad, standard mapping: left stick pans (the camera leaves the director), right stick Y and LT/RT zoom, A, B, X (captions), Y (Director or Follow), LB/RB (events), D-pad (focus, with auto-repeat). Text is at least 28 px at 1080p inside a 5% margin. The "Now watching" line names the followed creature or describes the view with the species as an italic slot. The god window's "TV remote" mini-panel for cast tabs is not done.
25. **God-window gamepad.** A clicks the focused control (or inspects the view centre), B is Esc, X Powers, Y the inspector, LB/RB the previous or next panel, D-pad moves focus (inside the top dialog if one is open), left stick pans, right stick or triggers zoom, or set intensity while a power is targeted.
26. **Accessibility.** axe-core 4.14.0 runs in Playwright over every god panel in both themes at 100% and 200% (see docs/spikes/ui-2.md): two findings fixed (`--faint` contrast, canvases outside landmarks), now zero. Added: a high-contrast theme (opaque panels, brighter lines and text), UI scale 75 to 200% (CSS zoom on the chrome; the camera fit accounts for it), a colour-blind-safe chart palette (Okabe and Ito) that replaces genetic hues in charts and the minimap only, and "Describe view" with a polite live region ("Viewing the twilight zone, 340 creatures, mostly *X*. Selected: ..."). Preferences persist per viewer; `?contrast=high` and `?scale=2` exist for tests.
27. **Real host link.** Today's `mod/host` STATS has no species list, so the client builds species from CENSUS (names from WELCOME's speciesTable) when STATS does not carry them. The e2e starts `dist/host.mjs` and attaches the god window and TV over the socket: both run without errors; commands answer `not-implemented` on this branch's host, and the UI says so per knob and keeps renames on the viewer.
28. **Dependency.** `axe-core@4.14.0` (exact, dev only, released 2026-10-05) is in `package.json` and `package-lock.json`. The shared `node_modules` got it by unpacking the npm tarball into `node_modules/axe-core` (it has no dependencies), not by `npm install`, so nothing else in the shared tree was touched.
