# Code of Conduct

Phylotank is a small, friendly project about watching life evolve. We want everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and anywhere else the project lives) to feel welcome and safe, whoever they are and however much they know.

This code is written for Phylotank and, like the project's other texts, is dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](ASSETS-LICENSE).

## What we expect

- **Be kind and patient.** Assume good intent. Many people here are learning: about evolution, about simulation, about open source, or about English.
- **Be constructive.** Criticise code, designs and ideas, never people. Say what would make a thing better.
- **Be honest.** Say what you know and what you do not. Credit other people's work, and do not pass off anyone else's creatures, art or text as your own (see "Original designs only" in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).
- **Respect people's time and limits.** Maintainers are volunteers. A "no" or a "not now" is an answer.
- **Keep it on topic** and suitable for a public, all-ages project.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or intimidation, in public or in private.
- Discrimination or demeaning remarks about anyone's identity, background, body, age, ability or beliefs.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or repeatedly pushing a point after being asked to stop.
- Encouraging or excusing any of the above.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, or you see this code broken, please tell the maintainers privately. On GitHub, contact the repository owner (currently [@TengilOurLiberator](https://github.com/TengilOurLiberator)), or use GitHub's "Report content" option on the comment or issue concerned. Reports are kept confidential, and nobody will be punished for a report made in good faith.

## What happens next

The maintainers will read every report, listen to everyone involved and decide what is fair. Depending on what happened, that may be a private word, a public request to stop, the removal of comments or contributions, or a temporary or permanent ban from the project's spaces. Maintainers who break this code are held to it like anyone else.

## Where it applies

In every space the project runs, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.
